    On making classes engaging
    Key takeaways
    • Numbers-heavy subjects hurt most. Accounts, Finance and Statistics top the list, mainly because of formulas and calculations.
    • Theory without practice loses students. Many say subjects feel boring or can't connect to real life.
    • VR is the top request. 14 students asked for VR, nearly half of all concrete ideas.
    • Hard-to-visualise topics fit VR well. Physics, EEE, case studies and simulations were named as places VR could help.

    Themes were grouped from open-ended answers. One answer can count under more than one theme. Source: VR Classroom Survey (55 responses).
